# Registry root keys

Procedure: `docs/root-key-custody.md`. Threshold: **3 of 5** (confirmed by
the Gravit Open Network Foundation, 2026-10-06).

Holders are appointed by the Board of the Gravit Open Network Foundation. Their identities
are recorded by the Foundation and disclosed to regulators and auditors on request; they are
not published, to reduce the risk of targeted attacks on key holders.

A registry is accepted only if at least 3 of the keys below have signed it.
No key in this table is active until its public key and proof of possession
are filled in and verified by two other holders (§4 of the procedure).

Rules that must hold once the table is filled in (§3 of the procedure):

- At most two holders from the same organisation; ideally at least two
  jurisdictions.
- No holder is also the only person able to merge registry pull requests.
- Each holder has signed a written acceptance of the procedure.
